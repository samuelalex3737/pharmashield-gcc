# Assumptions Register - PharmaShield GCC

Every assumption the model rests on is listed here. Each one is labelled "ASSUMPTION" in code comments and on the slide where it matters.
Status: Active (used in the final pipeline), Retired (replaced; kept for the audit trail).
"Source" says where the decision came from: Phase 0 audit fix (F), master plan PDF, Sam's 15 Nov overrides (O), or a notebook.

| ID | Assumption | Why it is needed | Where it is used | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | The client is a hypothetical UAE healthcare GPO, not any real organisation. | No public GCC GPO data exists. | All slides, app footer | Plan | Active |
| A2 | Lane behaviour learned from USAID SCMS shipments (ARV and HIV test kits, 2006-2015) transfers to injectable oncology makers through features: origin country, shipment mode, freight ratio, weight and site history. | SCMS has no carboplatin lines. | C2, C3 | F1, O2 | Active |
| A3 | Makers with no SCMS history receive the category average for C2 and C3. | Missing history is not bad history. | C2, C3 | F6 | Retired: C2 is now scored for every maker by model inference on its own profile (notebook 03); C3 uses the neutral population rate with a "no invoice history" label (A26, O8); probation is defined by A23. |
| A4 | US Medicaid 2025 reimbursement per unit (State Drug Utilization Data, national, non-suppressed rows) is a proxy for the relative price a GCC GPO would pay each maker. Makers with no Medicaid rows receive the median observed price and are flagged c1_imputed. | No public GCC tender prices by maker. | C1 | Phase 1, O6 | Active |
| A5 | Destination country is never a model feature because the UAE never appears in SCMS. | Avoid scoring outside the training distribution. | C2 | F5, O2 | Active |
| A6 | C4 = 0.4 inspection + 0.3 worst recall class + 0.2 NLP + 0.1 shortage. | Plan named inputs but no formula. | C4 | F6 | Retired: the master plan PDF formula is used instead (notebook 04), with the contract term replaced by the active-shortage flag (decision D9). |
| A7 | Compliance is assessed at the level of the companies that make the product (manufacturer entities in pipeline/reference/maker_entity_map.csv), not the whole corporate parent. | The NDC labeler is not the plant; a parent such as Pfizer has many unrelated plants. | C4 | F13, O3 | Active |
| A8 | Route mix by origin country (Gulf airspace, Hormuz sea lane, Gulf of Oman ports outside Hormuz) is a scenario assumption (pipeline/reference/route_mix_assumptions.csv). Crisis multiplies the Hormuz and Gulf air share by 1.5, capped at 1. | No public lane-level data for GCC pharma imports. | C5 | Plan, F9 | Active |
| A9 | LPI uses the World Bank 2023 edition, stored by the API under year 2022. | Latest edition available. | C5 | Phase 1 | Active |
| A10 | AHP pairwise judgements are made by the team role-playing the GPO board, one matrix per mode (Normal, Crisis, Cost focus), each with CR below 0.10 and no criterion below about 0.05. | Board priorities cannot be learned from data. | Weights | Plan, F7 | Active |
| A11 | Monthly carboplatin demand of the GPO is 10,000 vials of 45 mL. | No public GCC demand data. | MILP | Plan, O5 | Active |
| A12 | MILP objective = minimise sum of (landed cost + lambda x mean landed cost x (1 - S_risk)) x vials + 1,000,000 x spot-buy vials, where S_risk is the TOPSIS closeness on C2-C5 only and mean landed cost is over all 11 qualified makers. | Plan formula; cost counted once (D23). | MILP | Plan, O5, review fix 2 | Active |
| A13 | A probation maker may receive a trial order only if it has no OAI inspection and no Class I recall in the last 3 years. | Do not reward an unsafe maker with trial volume. | MILP | F6 | Active |
| A14 | Bulk-ingredient (API) labelers are excluded. Only finished-injection, human prescription listings with ANDA, NDA or BLA qualify: 11 makers. | A hospital cannot order finished injection from an API maker. | Qualification filter | Phase 1, O1 | Active |
| A15 | Recall searches use each labeler's distinctive name plus two aliases (AuroMedics for Eugia, Sicor for Teva). Fresenius Kabi Compounding is excluded. | Firm names vary across FDA datasets. | C4 | Phase 1 | Active |
| A16 | Recall counts are not used raw because large firms have more products. | Avoid penalising size. | C4 | Phase 1 | Active in modified form: the plan formula caps the recall term at 2 Class I/II recalls, so size beyond 2 recalls adds nothing. |
| A17 | KPI targets on slide 7 are team targets, not sourced figures. | Pilot targets are a management choice. | Slide 7 | Plan | Active |
| A18 | FDA inspection and recall data is used as a quality signal for makers that supply the GCC. It is US regulatory data. | GCC regulators do not publish an equivalent feed. | C4 | Plan, O11 | Active |
| A19 | The Kaggle copy of SCMS (10,324 rows x 33 columns) is a faithful copy of the USAID original. | The USAID portal no longer resolves. | Training layer | Phase 1 | Active |
| A20 | CFR section numbers in FDA inspection citations are weak labels for the NLP classifier (data integrity 211.68/180/186/188/194; sterility and contamination 211.28/42/46/56/67/113; process validation 211.100/110; quality unit 211.22/192/198; all other 211 sections = other GMP). A 12-citation hand-check agreed with these labels in 10 cases. | Hand-labelling at scale is not feasible in the timeline. | C4 NLP | F4, notebook 03 | Active |
| A21 | The order unit is the 450 mg / 45 mL vial. Landed cost per vial = price per mL x 45 x (1 + origin air freight ratio + origin insurance ratio), with ratios from SCMS air lines. | Medicaid prices are per mL; demand is in vials. | C1 | Notebook 02 | Active |
| A22 | Plant country = the most frequent country among the maker's matched FDA drug inspections; AVYXA has none, so its labeler home country (United States) is used. | The NDC directory does not name the plant. | C2, C5 | Notebook 02 | Active |
| A23 | Probation scenario: the GPO's incumbents are the makers with at least 5% of 2025 Medicaid carboplatin units (Hospira, Teva, Eugia, Fresenius Kabi, Accord: 98.3% of units). The other 6 makers are probation candidates. | O4: probation defined by a labelled, data-justified scenario. | MILP, app badges | O4, notebook 02 | Active |
| A24 | NLP severity weights: data integrity 1.0, sterility and contamination 1.0, quality unit 0.8, process validation 0.7, other GMP 0.4. | Plan says sterility and data integrity weigh most; the rest is team judgement. | C4 | Notebook 03 | Active |
| A25 | Carboplatin consignments to the GCC travel by air; each maker's C2 profile uses the median freight ratio and weight of SCMS air lines from its plant country. | Oncology injectables are high value per kg. | C2 | Notebook 03 | Active |
| A26 | Isolation Forest is fitted per molecule and dosage-form group with 50+ lines, on features relative to the group median, contamination 0.05. Makers without scored lines receive the population flag rate (0.0516) as a neutral C3 with a status label. | Prices are only comparable within a product. | C3 | F14, O8 | Active |
| A27 | Monthly capacity available to the GPO: 10,000 vials for an established maker, 2,000 for a probation maker. | No public capacity data. | MILP | O5 | Active |
| A28 | A maker with a current US shortage record has its available capacity cut by 20%. | A maker in shortage cannot promise full volume. | MILP | Notebook 04 | Active |
| A29 | Every order includes at least one eligible probation trial (5-15% of demand), and at most 2. | Pilot policy to build a qualified bench; O4 limit. | MILP | Plan, O4 | Active |
| A30 | An established maker that is selected receives at least 10% of demand (a warm backup), so dual sourcing is real, not nominal. | A selected backup with 0 vials gives no protection. | MILP | Notebook 04 | Active |
| A31 | Neutral-evidence rule: a maker with no FDA inspection in 5 years receives the panel median inspection term; a maker with inspections but no posted citations (or no inspection) receives the panel median NLP severity. | Missing evidence must not look like a clean record. | C4 | O8 logic, notebook 04 | Active |
| A32 | Carboplatin is Tier 1 (life-critical) because it is on the WHO Model List of Essential Medicines as a cytotoxic medicine. | Tier rules drive the MILP caps. | Tier rules | Plan | Active |
| A33 | Synthetic supply contracts (pipeline/reference/synthetic_contracts) are GenAI-written demonstrations; their clause risk is not used in any score. | No real GPO contracts are available. | Contract extractor demo | Phase 4 brief, D9 | Active |
| A34 | Compliance gate: an established maker with C4 compliance risk above 0.80 cannot be primary or backup unless no feasible split exists without it (then it is flagged). | Do not give the largest volumes to the makers with the worst recent FDA records. | MILP, app badges | Review fix 3, D24 | Active |
| A35 | Mode lambdas (price of risk): Normal 1.0, Crisis 2.0, Cost focus 0.5. Justification: a crisis doubles the price of risk because a stock-out costs more than the price premium. Sensitivity: the supplier split changes only at lambda 0.06 (Normal) and 0.05 (Crisis), where the India-made Gland trial gives way to AVYXA; from there to lambda 5 the split is Teva 70%, AVYXA 15%, Hospira 15% (notebook 04, section 4.5). | Each mode must be a full decision profile. | MILP, app | Review fixes 1 and 3; D21, D22, D33 | Active |
| A36 | East-coast reroute: in Crisis mode, sea volume that would cross the Strait of Hormuz is landed at Fujairah or Khor Fakkan (Gulf of Oman coast, outside the Strait) and trucked to Dubai, at a premium of 3.00 USD per rerouted vial and 3 extra days of lead time. Air volume through Gulf airspace cannot be rerouted this way. | No public lane-cost data; team estimate of extra port handling and overland trucking. | Route plan, KPIs, memo, route map | Third review fix 1, D31 | Active |
| A37 | Tier 1 backup rule: total probation share at most 15% of demand, and the backup share (established volume other than the primary) at least the total probation share. | A warm backup must be able to cover at least as much as all unproven trial suppliers together. | MILP (Python and TypeScript), contract validator | Third review fix 2, D32 | Active |
